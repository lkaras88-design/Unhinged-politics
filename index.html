30 deeply unserious questions. A frankly irresponsible amount of ideological inference. Several jokes would not survive a Senate confirmation hearing.

Designed by L. Karas

This is definitely a normal personality quiz and not evidence being assembled by a bipartisan commission.

We will ask about shopping carts, dinner checks, ugly lawns, cursed antiques, self-checkout machines, group chats, and other issues the Founders cowardly refused to address.

The premise: mundane choices often reveal deeper instincts about fairness, authority, freedom, tradition, community, expertise, rules, and who should be trusted to make decisions. Also, occasionally, whether you would survive a neighborhood Facebook group. This is satire—not a validated political-science instrument, diagnosis, voter guide, or proof that your lawn opinions determine your ballot.

Answer instinctively. The questions and answer choices shuffle so you cannot easily reverse-engineer what we're accusing you of. Please do not consult your attorney until after Question 30.
