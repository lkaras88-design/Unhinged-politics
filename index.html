This is definitely a normal personality quiz and not a federal investigation.

We will ask about shopping carts, dinner checks, ugly lawns, roommates, family recipes, suspicious vans, self-checkout machines, and other issues the Founders cowardly refused to address.

The premise: mundane choices often reveal deeper instincts about fairness, authority, freedom, tradition, community, expertise, rules, and who should be trusted to make decisions. This is satire—not a validated political-science instrument, diagnosis, voter guide, or proof that your lawn opinions determine your ballot.

Answer instinctively. The questions and answer choices will shuffle so you cannot easily reverse-engineer what we're accusing you of.
